Nearby places, filtered for the walk ahead

Find places within the walk time you choose.

WayNear helps you find nearby places that are close enough to walk to and, when opening-hour data is available, stay open long enough for what you need.

What WayNear does

  • Choose your starting point Use your current location, type a place or address, or choose a point on the map.
  • Set how long you want to walk Search by actual walking time instead of guessing from straight-line distance.
  • Pick the kind of place you need Find restaurants, cafés, shops, pharmacies, bars, hotels and many other place types.
  • Filter by opening time Show places that are open now and set a minimum amount of time they need to remain open.
  • Set a minimum rating Remove places below the rating threshold you choose before they reach the result list.
  • Get a focused result list WayNear returns up to 10 matching places and lets you compare the results that meet your criteria.
  • Open the place in Google Maps View the place or hand off to Google Maps for walking directions when you are ready to go.
  • Built for quick decisions Designed for the moment when you care about walking distance, opening time and whether a place fits right now.
